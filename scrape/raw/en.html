Eat Local - Eat Fresh Arbor Restaurant is located in the very heart of Bled centre. The social environment of the interior and the terrace is vibrant and inviting. Along with the top quality meal, you can also take in many attractions such as Lake Bled, Bled Island and the Castle. We offer Italian, Mediterranean and Slovenian cuisine following contemporary trends with the emphasis on healthy and light diet. The Restaurant is also suitable for larger meetings and events as it can accommodate up to 55 guests in the interior and 50 on the terrace. "Nothing arouses memories better than taste."
